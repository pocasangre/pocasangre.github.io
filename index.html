About

Oscar Pocasangre is Senior Data Analyst in the Political Reform program at New America, where he focuses on electoral systems and democratic politics. He has written extensively on proportional representation systems, fusion voting, the age divide in American politics, and direct democracy. He is also affiliated with The Guinier Project at Harvard Law School, doing research on minority representation under alternative electoral systems and the policy and governance consequences of proportional representation systems.

Pocasangre holds a PhD in political science from Columbia University, where he focused on comparative politics and quantitative methods. He previously worked for the MIT Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL) in Santiago, Chile as a Policy and Training Analyst. He earned an MPA from Columbia and a BA from Yale.
